The Origin

We watched a great gym owner lose 40 members in one month. Not because of his gym — because of his tools.

In 2024, we met a gym owner in Tehran running a genuinely excellent club. Clean, well-equipped, great trainers, loyal members. But he operated entirely manually — Telegram messages answered when he got to them, no churn monitoring, no lead system, no data dashboard.

In one month, he lost 40 members. Not because they disliked his gym. Because a lead didn't get a reply in time and joined elsewhere. Because a member stopped coming and nobody noticed until their membership expired. Because renewals slipped through with no reminder. Every single loss was preventable.

The technology to fix this existed. It was just built for global markets with different channels, different food culture, and different business reality. IKONIQ is what we built instead — from scratch, for Iran, for how Iranian health clubs actually operate.
